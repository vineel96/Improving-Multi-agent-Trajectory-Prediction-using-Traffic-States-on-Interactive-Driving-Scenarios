1. Overview of Winograd Convolution
Winograd's minimal filtering algorithm is based on polynomial factorization and the Chinese Remainder Theorem. The goal is to transform the inputs and kernel into a space where the number of multiplications is minimized. The convolution problem is reduced to a series of polynomial multiplications, which is why the transformation matrices come into play.
